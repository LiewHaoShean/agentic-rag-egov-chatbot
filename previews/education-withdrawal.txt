Education Withdrawal
Finance Your Higher Education
Learn How To Apply (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/d/guest/panduan-mudah-pengeluaran-pendidikan)
Quality education is essential for career stability and a better life for you and your family. You can choose to withdraw savings from your Akaun Sejahtera to help fund education for yourself, your spouse, children, and/or parents at approved institutions, both locally and abroad.
What You Can Withdraw
Total tuition fees/outstanding education loan OR entire savings in Akaun Sejahtera (whichever is lower)
What's Covered
Tuition fees and charges imposed by the higher learning institution OR outstanding loan amount
For Certificate Level 3, withdrawal eligibility only applies to tuition fees subject to the total savings balance in Akaun Sejahtera
Hostel & accommodation Fees
One-way flight for first-year students studying outstation/ abroad
Who Can Apply
Malaysians & Non-Malaysians
Below 55 years of age
Have savings in Akaun Sejahtera

Education Withdrawal - FAQ
How frequent can member's make a withdrawal?
Members are allowed to make a withdrawal anytime as long as there is sufficient balance in their Akaun Sejahtera (subject to withdrawal terms).
What is the total amount allowed for withdrawal?
The total tuition fees or the balance in your Akaun Sejahtera, whichever is lower.
Why is thumbprint verification necessary?

--- CHUNK 15 ---
Why is thumbprint verification necessary?
Members need to be present at the EPF office and verify their thumbprint as proof of identity as a member and applicant.
What happens if a member does not provide thumbprint verification?
Members are given 14 days to verify their thumbprint, failing which the application will be rejected.
Does the member need to produce any document for thumbprint verification at the EPF office?
Original identification card (MyKad/MyPR/Police ID/Military ID) for member‘s identity verification
Supporting documents – members' will be notified of any additional documents through i-Akaun inbox
Where can members perform the thumbprint verification and submit the supporting documents?
Members can perform a thumbprint verification and submit the supporting documents at any EPF offices or at Self-Service Terminal (SST) for any application without document.

How often can members make a withdrawal to reduce/settle their education loan balance?

--- CHUNK 18 ---
How often can members make a withdrawal to reduce/settle their education loan balance?
Members can apply for this withdrawal at any time, as long as there is a remaining balance on their study loan with the education loan provider and the member still has savings in their Akaun Sejahtera.
Can members pay the remaining education loan balance to the education loan provider on a monthly basis?
No, a lump sum payment is made to the education loan provider for each withdrawal application.
How much can a member withdraw for payment?
The amount of payment that can be withdrawn is subject to the member’s loan balance and the balance savings in their Akaun Sejahtera, whichever is lower.
Will the education loan provider have to repay any excess amount withdrawn back to the EPF in the event the member’s loan has already been settled?
The education loan provider will need to reimburse any excess amount withdrawn to the EPF is the loan has already been settled.

--- CHUNK 19 ---
Can members make a withdrawal to settle their education loan balance if they are still studying at the higher learning institution?
Yes, members will need to submit the documents listed in the document checklist, as well as their loan balance statement. Application submissions must be done at an EPF office only.
What are the enhancements under EPF’s Education Withdrawal?
Education withdrawals have been expanded as follows:
i.
Withdrawal covers Certificate Level 3:
Withdrawal applications are extended to Certificate Level 3, subject to the list of certifications approved by the EPF; and
ii.
Withdrawal to finance/reduce education loans for spouses and/or parents:
Withdrawal applications are extended to finance tuition fees and/or to reduce the education loans of spouses (husband/wife) and parents (biological/adopted/step parents) for Certificate Level 3, Diploma and above.
What is Certificate Level 3?

--- CHUNK 20 ---
What is Certificate Level 3?
Certificate Level 3 is the Malaysian Skills Certificate (SKM) accredited by the Department of Skills Development/Jabatan Pembangunan Kemahiran (DSD/JPK), Ministry of Human Resources for skills programmes offered by public or private Training Providers or ‘Accredited Centers’.
What are the objectives of expanding Education Withdrawal to include Certificate Level 3?
The objectives to expand the Education Withdrawal to include Certificate Level 3 are as follows:
i. To provide opportunities for more Members/students to further their education;
ii. To encourage upskilling and reskilling in line with the aspirations of the Industrial Revolution (IR 4.0);
iii. To assist related industries in producing quality workers who will contribute to economic growth and resilience in the future; and

--- CHUNK 21 ---
iv. To enable members, spouses, children, and parents to increase their level of education, thereby allowing them to earn a higher income and enjoy a better standard of living while increasing their retirement savings in the EPF.
Are Members eligible to apply for this Education Withdrawal for all Certificate Level 3 courses/programmes conducted at any Accredited Centre (AC) or Training Provider?
Currently, Members are only eligible to apply for this withdrawal for Certificate Level 3 courses/programmes offered by Accredited Centres (Pusat Bertauliah/PB) and Training Providers that are accredited by the Department of Skills Development (JPK) only.

What is the amount of withdrawal allowed under Education Withdrawal?
The withdrawal amount allowed is the total tuition fees (academic year) or the balance of the education loan subject to savings in Akaun Sejahtera (whichever is lower).
For a joint withdrawal application, the amount of withdrawal allowed must not exceed the total tuition fee.
Can members/students pursuing Certificate Level 3 studies claim reimbursement of tuition fees?

--- CHUNK 25 ---
Can members/students pursuing Certificate Level 3 studies claim reimbursement of tuition fees?
Yes, members/students can make reimbursement of tuition fees provided that the receipt date does not exceed one year when the application was made and is subject to the member’s Akaun Sejahtera balance.
For reimbursements in more than one receipt, the total withdrawal amount is subject to the member’s Akaun Sejahtera balance.
Payment will be credited to the member’s Savings/Current Bank Account of choice.

For payment using banker's cheque, members will receive the notice of payment via mail to the member’s address.

--- CHUNK 26 ---
Will members be notified if payment is made to an Accredited Centre?
Yes, members will be notified with regards to payment made to Accredited Centre via a notice posted to the member's address.
If members submit an offer letter for Certificate Level 1, 2, & 3 studies, can the withdrawal application be processed if members have only registered for Certificate Level 1?
No. Members are only eligible to apply for Education Withdrawal if the offer letter confirms that they have started studying for a Certificate Level 3 only.
