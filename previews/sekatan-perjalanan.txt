If a taxpayer is unable to pay in full, a temporary release can be applied with the following conditions:
The taxpayer / representative may walk in / Contact HASiL Office that handles the taxpayer’s file.
Submitting an application letter that include the destination information, purpose, and the duration of visit.
Make a payment 50% of outstanding tax amount. Refer methods of payment as above
Temporary release letter will be issued by specifying the period of temporary stoppage granted.
Receipt of payment and application to be submitted 5 days before the travelling date.
