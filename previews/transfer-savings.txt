Transfer Savings Between Own EPF Accounts
Members can transfer all or part of their savings from Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) to Akaun Persaraan (Account 1) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2).
This initiative was implemented to facilitate members in exercising self-discipline in managing their savings, with the aim of securing retirement readiness and addressing pre-retirement life cycle needs.
Who Is Eligible To Apply
Malaysian and Non-Malaysian
Below 55 years of age
Have savings balance in Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) or Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3)
Have new contributions credited to Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) or Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3)

Transfer Savings Between Own EPF Account - FAQ
What is meant by the transfer between own EPF accounts?

--- CHUNK 3 ---
What is meant by the transfer between own EPF accounts?
Members can transfer all or part of their savings from Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) to Akaun Persaraan (Account 1) and Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2). The transfer of savings only involves the member's own EPF account and transferred savings cannot be returned to the original account.
Why was the transfer between own EPF accounts introduced?
This initiative was implemented to facilitate members in exercising self-discipline in managing their savings, with the aim of securing retirement readiness and addressing pre-retirement life cycle needs. The transfer will not affect the dividend payments on the savings.
Who is eligible to transfer between EPF accounts?
EPF members who are eligible to transfer savings between their own EPF accounts are as follows:
i.   Malaysian and Non Malaysian; AND
ii.  Below 55 years of age; AND
iii. Have savings balance in Akaun Sejahtera or Akaun Fleksibel; OR

Yes, the following combinations of transfers are permitted:
i. Akaun Fleksibel to Akaun Sejahtera AND Akaun Persaraan; or

What category of savings transfer are available?
There are two (2) category of savings transfers:
i. One-Time Transfer
ii. Recurring Transfer
What is meant by One-Time Transfer?
One-Time Transfer is the transfer of
available savings balance
from Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) to Akaun Persaraan (Account 1) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2).
Application must be made for each transfer
, subject to eligible balance at the time of application.
For One-Time Transfer, what is the eligible amount that can be transferred?

--- CHUNK 6 ---
For One-Time Transfer, what is the eligible amount that can be transferred?
The eligible amount that can be transferred is the current balance after deducting any pending approval of withdrawals and/or unpaid approved withdrawals by the EPF (if any) on the date of the transfer application.
Transfer of savings can be made entirely or partially. For transfers of full savings, the total amount will be transferred. For partial transfers, the transferred amount will exclude any cents.
What is the frequency of One-Time Transfer that members can make?
One-Time Transfer can be done at any time, subject to eligible balance at the time of application.
For One-Time Transfer, when will the transferred amount be updated in the member's account ?
The transferred amount will be updated immediately to the account of the member involved. Meanwhile, the contribution transfer information will be displayed in the member's statement the next day.

What is meant by Recurring Transfer?
Recurring Transfer is a transfer of
new contributions
from Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2) to Akaun Persaraan (Akaun 1) and/or Akaun Sejahtera (Akaun 2) based on the
percentage (%)
of transfer chosen by the member.
Members need to submit an application only once and the recurring transfer will be automatically executed for each new contribution credited continuously until the member makes a cancellation or changes the existing transfer percentage.

Can members change their Recurring Transfer percentage?

--- CHUNK 9 ---
Can members change their Recurring Transfer percentage?
Yes, members can change their Recurring Transfer percentage (%) at any time according to their needs and preferences. The updated percentage will take effect immediately and cancels the previously selected percentage.
Can members who have made One-Time Transfer apply for a Recurring Transfer and vice versa?
Yes, members can choose One-Time Transfer and Recurring Transfer subject to the stipulated terms and conditions.
Can members cancel Recurring Transfer?
Members can cancel Recurring Transfer at any time through the KWSP i-Akaun app, i-Akaun (Member) web portal, and Self-Service Terminals (SST) at EPF Offices. The cancellation of the transfer will take effect immediately.
Can Recurring Transfer be cancelled automatically?
Recurring Transfer will be automatically cancelled under the following circumstances:
i. Member reaches 55 years of age.
ii. Approved application for full withdrawal:
- Death Withdrawal

i. Member reaches 55 years of age.
ii. Approved application for full withdrawal:
- Death Withdrawal
- Leaving Country Withdrawal
- Pensionable Employees Withdrawal
- Incapacitation Withdrawal
Besides the KWSP i-Akaun app, i-Akaun (Member) web portal and SST channels, can members submit an application for transfer of savings at the EPF office?
One-Time Transfer: YES
Members can apply for a
One-Time Transfer
at the EPF office by completing and submitting Form
KWSP 12 - Savings Transfer To Akaun Persaraan/Akaun Sejahtera.
However, members are encouraged to submit their applications online via the KWSP i-Akaun app, i-Akaun (Member) web portal, or SST.
Recurring Transfer: NO
Applications for registration and cancellation of Recurring Transfers can only be submitted through the KWSP i-Akaun app, i-Akaun (Member) web portal, or SST.
