Akaun Persaraan Top-Up Savings
A Little Savings Set Aside Today Goes A Long Way
An option for you to contribute to the retirement wellbeing of your loved ones. With this facility, you (the topper) can voluntarily make additional contributions to your family members' (the toppee) EPF accounts.
Who Can Apply
Asset Publisher
Death Benefit
Subject to EPF terms and conditions.
Tax Exemption
Subject to IRB terms and conditions.
Annual Dividend
Enjoy annual dividends on top of your retirement savings.
Special Incentive
Up to 15% of total contribution or max RM300 this year.
Who Can Contribute?
Children (to parents)
Husband (to wife)
Wife (to husband)
Parents (to children)
Requirements
Toppee
EPF Member
Malaysian citizen & permanent resident
Below age 55
Topper
EPF or non-EPF Member
Toppers need to register before making payment by filling in the Pendaftaran Penambah Simpanan/ Penerima Simpanan form -
KWSP 3B (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/20119/44767/Form_KWSP_3B.pdf)

Contribution Limit

The maximum accumulated limit for Voluntary Contributions (i-Simpan Contribution, i‑Saraan Contribution, i‑Saraan Plus Contribution, i‑Suri Contribution and Akaun Persaraan Top‑up Savings Contribution) across all payment channels.
Important Reminder
