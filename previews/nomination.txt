What is EPF nomination?
Nomination is a declaration made by a member to appoint or name a person as a beneficiary, or as a
wasi

--- CHUNK 9 ---
wasi
(executor) or administrator (for Muslim members), to administer the member’s EPF savings upon the member’s death.
Why do I need to make a nomination?
Members are encouraged to make a nomination to facilitate and expedite the withdrawal of their EPF savings by the nominated person(s) in the event of death. Without a nomination, heirs or next-of-kin may face difficulties or delays in claiming the deceased member's EPF savings.
How can I make a nomination?
Members can make or update their nomination through the following channels:
1. KWSP i-Akaun App
Identity verification can be completed via *e-KYC, without the need to visit an EPF office.
2. i-Akaun (Member) Web Portal or Nomination Form (KWSP 4)
Members are required to visit any EPF office for thumbprint verification (CIJ).
Any new nomination will automatically cancel all previous nominations.
*Subject to terms and conditions.

When will my nomination be effective?
The effective date of a nomination depends on the nomination method and verification process.
| Nomination Method | Effective Date |
| KWSP Form 4/ i-Akaun (Member) Web Portal | The nomination takes effect on the date the member completes thumbprint verification at any EPF office. |
| KWSP i-Akaun App (e-KYC facial recognition verification) | The nomination takes effect two (2) days after the registration date. |
Can next-of-kin claim EPF savings if the member has named someone else as a nominee?
No. According to the EPF Act, payment will only be made to the person(s) named by the member as EPF beneficiary(ies).
Who can be named as a nominee?
EPF members may nominate an individual or an approved institution to manage their EPF savings in the event of their death. The nomination arrangement differs for Muslim and non-Muslim members.

What are the responsibilities of a nominee?
The responsibilities of a nominee depend on the member’s religion.
The nominee acts as a wasi (administrator) responsible for distributing the deceased member’s EPF savings to the rightful beneficiaries in accordance with Islamic law.

--- CHUNK 12 ---
The nominee is the rightful beneficiary of the deceased member’s EPF savings.
If the nominee predeceases the member, the rightful applicants are the surviving next-of-kin of the deceased member.
If the member predeceases the nominee, the rightful applicant is the nominee’s next-of-kin.

What does identity verification via e-KYC mean for nomination registration through the KWSP i-Akaun app?
Identity verification via e-KYC is the process of electronically verifying a member’s identity using technologies such as Facial Recognition (FR), which allows EPF to ensure that the member’s information is accurate and valid without the need to be physically present at an EPF office.
Do I need to visit an EPF office if I use e-KYC verification?
If e-KYC verification is
successful
: The member is not required to visit the EPF office as the identity has been verified digitally.
If e-KYC verification
fails
: The member is required to visit the EPF office to complete the identity verification process using thumbprint verification (CIJ).
How long does a member have to complete thumbprint verification for an online EPF nomination if e-KYC verification fails?

If e-KYC verification fails, the member has 30 days from the date the nomination is submitted to complete thumbprint verification at any EPF office.
When does a nomination registered through the KWSP i-Akaun app take effect after successful e-KYC verification?
The nomination will take effect after the Cooling Period (T+1) has ended. Below is an example for reference:
| Scenario | Cooling Period | Effective Date |
| A nomination is submitted by the member and e-KYC verification is successfully completed on 1 Dec 2025 | 01 Dec 2025 – 02 Dec 2025 | 03 Dec 2025 |
Does a nomination become effective for Death Withdrawal if the member passes away during the cooling period (T+1)?
No. The nomination does not take effect if the member passes away during the cooling period (T+1), as the cooling period has not yet ended.

Can a nomination application within the Cooling Period (T+1) be cancelled?
Yes. The member can cancel the nomination online via the KWSP i-Akaun app or in person at any EPF office during the Cooling Period (T+1).
