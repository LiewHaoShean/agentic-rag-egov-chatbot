i-Saraan
Retirement Savings For Self‑Employed Members
Learn How To Register (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/d/guest/easy-guide-i-saraan-registration_eng)
i-Saraan Plus is a voluntary contribution initiative by EPF, designed specifically for e‑hailing and p‑hailing drivers. The program allows these members to receive special incentives from the Government to enhance their retirement security, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
Benefits
Special Incentive
Receive a special incentive of 20% (up to a maximum of RM600 per year) on the total voluntary contributions made for the current year*
Annual Dividend
Enjoy annual dividends on top of retirement savings
Death Assistance
Subject to EPF terms and conditions
Tax Relief
Subject to IRB terms and conditions
*Lifetime incentive capped at RM6,000 or until the member reaches age 60, whichever comes first, subject to the Government’s terms and conditions.

General - i-Saraan Plus
What is i-Saraan Plus?
i-Saraan Plus is an enhancement of the existing i-Saraan programme under EPF's voluntary contributions, introduced starting 2026.
It provides an opportunity for members working as e-hailing and/or p-hailing drivers to receive higher Government incentives of up to
RM600 per year
and
a lifetime total of RM6,000
for retirement security purposes, subject to established terms and conditions.
With the introduction of i-Saraan Plus, will i-Saraan still continue?
Yes
. The i-Saraan programme will continue for self-employed individuals and those in the informal sector, such as small business owners, freelancers, and individuals outside the formal workforce.

If a member has already registered for i-Saraan, do they need to re-register for i-Saraan Plus?
No. Members who have already registered under the i-Saraan programme do not need to register again.
However, participation in i-Saraan Plus is subject to eligibility. Only members who work as e‑hailing and/or p‑hailing drivers are eligible to join i-Saraan Plus.
What is e-hailing and p-hailing?
e-hailing
e-hailing refers to the service of transporting passengers using private vehicles. Bookings are made through electronic mobile applications that connect drivers with passengers needing transportation.
p-hailing

--- CHUNK 9 ---
p-hailing
p-hailing refers to the service of delivering food, drinks, or parcels. Orders are received via mobile applications, requiring the rider to collect items from a vendor and deliver them to a customer.
How can a member join i-Saraan Plus?
Participation in i-Saraan Plus is automatic once a member makes a voluntary contribution through their respective service provider under the e-hailing and/or p-hailing sector.
Can public service employees under the pensionable service scheme participate in i-Saraan Plus?
Yes. Public service employees who are currently employed under the pensionable service scheme and are under the age of 60 are eligible to participate in i-Saraan Plus, provided they are working as e-hailing and/or p-hailing drivers. The voluntary EPF contributions must be made through their respective service providers under the e-hailing and/or p-hailing sector.
Can members who are currently serving in the military or military retirees participate in i-Saraan Plus?

Yes, military members who are still serving or military retirees are eligible to participate in i-Saraan Plus, provided they are working as e-hailing and/or p-hailing drivers. The voluntary EPF contributions must be made through their respective service providers under the e-hailing and/or p-hailing sector.

What is the i-Saraan Plus special incentive amount?
The i-Saraan Plus special incentive rate is 20% (up to a maximum of RM600 per year) of the total voluntary contributions received in the current year*.
*Lifetime incentive limit of RM6,000 or upon reaching the age of 60, whichever comes first (subject to terms and conditions).
What is the contribution amount required to receive the maximum i-Saraan Plus special incentive of RM600 per year?
Members qualify for the special incentive based on a matching contribution of 20% (up to a maximum of RM600) of the total voluntary contributions made by the member in the current year. To receive the maximum special incentive of RM600 per year, members must contribute at least RM3,000 per year under i-Saraan Plus.

When does the i-Saraan Plus lifetime special incentive limit of RM6,000 come into effect?
The i-Saraan Plus lifetime special incentive limit of RM6,000 applies to contributions made through service providers under the e-hailing and/or p-hailing sector, starting from 2026 for members newly qualify for i-Saraan Plus.
For existing members who qualify for i-Saraan Plus and have previously received i-Saraan incentives, the lifetime special incentive limit will be increased to RM6,000, calculated based on incentives received since 2024.
Can i-Saraan Plus participants eligible to receive the special i-Saraan incentive and the i-Saraan Plus incentive at the same time?
No. Members are only eligible to receive one (1) incentive in the current year.

When will the i-Saraan Plus special incentive payment be credited into the member's EPF account?
The i-Saraan Plus special incentive payment will be credited to the member's EPF account twice a year (subject to the receipt of special incentive payments from the Government).
Can members apply for a refund of i-Saraan Plus contributions that have been made to EPF?
No. All i-Saraan Plus contributions paid to the EPF are final. Therefore, members must provide consent to the service provider before the contribution payment is made.
How is the i-Saraan Plus contribution credited into members' account?
i-Saraan Plus contributions will be credited into Akaun Persaraan (Account 1), Akaun Sejahtera (Account 2), and Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3) according to the distribution rates of 75%, 15%, and 10%.
For members who have reached the age of 55, all i-Saraan Plus contributions will be credited into Akaun Emas.

--- CHUNK 14 ---
How is the i-Saraan Plus special incentive credited into members' accounts?
The i-Saraan Plus special incentive will be credited into Akaun Persaraan (Account 1) for members below the age of 55. For members who have reached the age of 55, the special incentive will be credited into Akaun Emas.
What is i-Saraan?
i-Saraan is a voluntary contribution facility introduced by EPF. It allows self-employed members, individuals with no fixed income, and gig economy sector workers to contribute towards their retirement savings while receiving a government special incentive, subject to the terms and conditions set by EPF.
What improvements has the government announced for i-Saraan?
The government has increased the special incentive rate for i-Saraan from 15% to 20% to encourage more Malaysians, especially those in the informal sector or without a fixed income, to save for retirement.

--- CHUNK 15 ---
However, the maximum annual special incentive remains RM500 per year and RM5,000 for a lifetime, or until the member reaches the age of 60, whichever comes first.
What are the requirements to apply for i-Saraan?
To apply for i-Saraan, the member must meet the following requirements:
Registered as an EPF member
Malaysian citizen
Self-employed individuals with no fixed income, civil service members under a pension scheme, or workers in the gig economy sector
Can members who have reached the age of 55 register i-Saraan?
Yes. EPF members who are Malaysian citizens and have not reached the age of 60 can register for i-Saraan.
Can members who have reached the age of 60 register i-Saraan?
No. i-Saraan is only open to EPF members who have not reached the age of 60.
Can members who are civil service employees under a pension scheme apply for i-Saraan?
Yes. Civil service employees who are under the pension scheme and below the age of 60 are eligible to register for i-Saraan.

--- CHUNK 16 ---
Can members who are currently serving in the military apply for i-Saraan?
Yes. Military members who are still serving can apply for i-Saraan if they are registered as EPF members.
What is the current i-Saraan special incentive rate?
The i-Saraan special incentive rate is 20% of the total voluntary contributions made in the current year, up to a maximum of RM500 per year.
The special incentive is subject to a lifetime maximum of RM5,000 or until the member reaches the age of 60, whichever comes first.
Subject to terms and conditions.
When does the lifetime special incentive maximum limit of RM5,000 take effect?
The RM5,000 lifetime special incentive limit takes effect from 2024, based on contributions made from 2024 onwards.
How is the lifetime maximum limit calculated?
The lifetime maximum limit is calculated based on the cumulative total of annual Special Incentives and is limited to RM5,000 or upon reaching the age of 60, whichever comes first.

What are the benefits of i-Saraan?
Members who contribute under i-Saraan can obtain the following benefits:
Increase retirement savings with government special incentives
Earn annual dividends on EPF savings
Death benefit (subject to EPF terms and conditions)
Tax relief eligibility (subject to IRB terms and conditions)

When will the i-Saraan special incentive be credited to the member’s EPF account?
The i-Saraan special incentive will be credited to the member’s EPF account twice a year, subject to the receipt of special incentive payments from the Government.
Can members request a refund for i-Saraan contribution payment?
No. All i-Saraan contributions paid to EPF are final. Members should ensure that the payment details are correct before completing the transaction.
What happens if i-Saraan contributions are made after the age of 55?
All i-Saraan contributions received after the member reaches 55 years old will be credited into Akaun Emas and can only be withdrawn once the member reaches the age of 60.
