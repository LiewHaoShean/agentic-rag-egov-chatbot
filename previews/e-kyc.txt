1. What is the aim of this initiative?
This initiative aims to expand EPF’s coverage by allowing individuals to become EPF members more easily. It simplifies and accelerates the registration process where applicants are not required to physically visit an EPF Office.
2. Who is this initiative for?
This initiative is for;
a. Those who have yet to register as EPF members.
b. Malaysian citizens or permanent residents owning a MyKad or MyPR.
c. Applicants aged between 14 years and has not yet reached 75 years old.
3. How can I register to be an EPF member through this facility?
Step 1: Download the KWSP i-Akaun app via:
a) Apple App Store; or
b) Google Play Store; or
c) Huawei App Gallery
Step 2: Select the option '
Not yet register as member or i-Akaun?
'.
Step 3: Complete the electronic Know-Your-Customer (e-KYC) process and key in the required information.
4. What is the e-KYC process and why is it required?

--- CHUNK 6 ---
4. What is the e-KYC process and why is it required?
The e-KYC process, short for 'electronic Know-Your-Customer', refers to an electronic identity verification process via face recognition technology through the KWSP i-Akaun app. This process is required to verify the applicant’s identity before activating the EPF member account.
5. What must be done to ensure that registration as an EPF member is successful?
For a successful EPF member registration, please ensure the following:
a)    Please use the original identification documents (MyKad or MyPR). Identification documents in the form of copies/ digital/ photos are not accepted.
b)    Make sure to use a phone that has a front camera function.
c)    Ensure that you are in a well-lit area with stable internet connection.
6. Does the applicant need to submit additional documents to EPF in order for EPF to verify the applicant’s information?

--- CHUNK 7 ---
No, there is no need for the applicant to submit any additional documents. The system will automatically verify the applicant’s information by matching it with the information provided.
7. Does the applicant need to physically visit the EPF Office to get the application approved?
No, there is no need for the applicant visit an EPF office. Application will be approved in real time.
8. What if the applicant fails to register as an EPF member through this facility?
If applicant fails to register through this online facility, they will have to register at an EPF Office.
9. Is it possible for another party, such as a husband registering on behalf of his wife, to complete a member registration for others through this facility?
No, registration must be done by the actual applicant.

11. Are there specific requirements for the mobile phone used for member registration?
The mobile phone used for the purpose of member registration must have front-facing camera function and access to stable internet connection.
12. Can member registration be done using laptop?
No. Registration can only be done through a mobile phone that has a front camera function. This is to ensure the image quality is adequate.

5. Do I need to uninstall the i-Akaun (classic) after downloading the KWSP i-Akaun on my device?
No. You are not required to uninstall the i-Akaun (classic).
However, we encourage you to switch and use KWSP i-Akaun for a more personalised and user-friendly experience.
6. Do I need to create a new user ID and password for KWSP i-Akaun?
For members who already have the i-Akaun (classic), you can log in to KWSP i-Akaun by using the same user ID and password.
However, new users need to create a new user ID and password before logging in to KWSP i-Akaun.
7. If I am an EPF member and want to register for i-Akaun, can I do it through KWSP i-Akaun?
Yes. You can register through KWSP i-Akaun.
8. Can I download and use both the i-Akaun (classic) and KWSP i-Akaun at the same time?
Yes. You can still use the i-Akaun (classic). However, the app can only be used until a certain date in the future before it is discontinued.

--- CHUNK 13 ---
Therefore, we encourage you to switch to KWSP i-Akaun for a more personalised and user-friendly experience.
9. If I uninstall the i-Akaun (classic), will all my details be erased?
No. All your details will be retained. Once you log in with the same username and password, all your existing information will be synced automatically to KWSP i-Akaun.

Yes. You can update your profile information through KWSP i-Akaun.

4. Can I cancel withdrawals through KWSP i-Akaun?
Yes. You can cancel the withdrawal application through KWSP i-Akaun if the status of your application is still "Submitted".
5. Do I still need to submit documents at the EPF office if I make withdrawal application through KWSP i-Akaun?
No. You no longer need to visit the EPF office to submit documents for withdrawal application if you apply through KWSP i-Akaun.
However, please note that you may still need to do so for thumbprint verification if any of the following conditions applies:

6. How can KWSP i-Akaun assist me in planning for a better retirement?
You can plan for your retirement effectively by utilising the Retirement Calculator and exploring our articles on retirement planning in KWSP i-Akaun.
7. Can I revoke my Simpanan Shariah registration using the KWSP i-Akaun?
Yes. You can make a revocation request through KWSP i-Akaun before the Simpanan Shariah takes effect. The effective date will be on 1st January of the year following the registration, subject to allocation of annual Shariah fund on first come first served basis and any other conditions as determined by the Board.

8. What is the purpose of the ‘Forget This Device' function in the profile section of KWSP i-Akaun?
The ‘Forget This Device’ security feature logs you out of KWSP i-Akaun. Once you're logged out, you will need to re-enter your user ID and password to access the app again.

2. How can I ensure the security of my transactions made through KWSP i-Akaun?
Transactions through KWSP i-Akaun are secured with the following elements:
a. Authorisation of transactions is done through the Transaction Authorisation Code (TAC) received by the member via their phone number registered with the EPF.
b. Access is granted using biometrics and password authentication.
c. Mobile binding is the process of registering your device as a trusted device to perform transactions in KWSP i-Akaun.
3. What are the available channels for me to contact if I encounter any issues accessing KWSP i-Akaun?
The following channels are available for you to visit or access:
a. EPF Website
b. WhatsApp
c. EPF Offices
d. Mobile KWSP
4. Is it possible to continue using the i-Akaun (classic) in the future?
Yes, while the i-Akaun (classic) remains accessible, it will not receive further updates until it is phased out.

5. If I perform transactions in KWSP i-Akaun, will they be updated in the i-Akaun (classic) as well?
Yes. If any transactions or functions are performed through KWSP i-Akaun, the same transaction records will be updated in the i-Akaun (classic), and vice versa.
