--- CHUNK 0 ---
Reduce or Redeem Housing Loan Withdrawal
Speed Up Your House Ownership Journey
You can make withdrawals from Akaun Sejahtera to reduce/redeem your housing loan balance or assist your spouse with paying off theirs.
Who Can Apply
Requirements
Malaysians & Non-Malaysians
Below 55 years of age
At least RM500 in Akaun Sejahtera
Bought/built first or second residential house*
Registered owner of the property
(Self Withdrawal only)
Residential house has been charged as collateral
Have an outstanding housing loan with loan providers recognised by the EPF
The application is made 1 year after the date of the previous Reduce/Redeem Housing Loan Withdrawal
Approved loan from recognised borrowers**
*Residential property – including bungalow, terrace, semi‑detached, apartment, condominium, studio apartment, serviced apartment, townhouse, Small Office Home Office (SOHO), or shophouse with a residential unit, and other similar residential property types in Malaysia.

--- CHUNK 1 ---
**Certified financial institutions under the Financial Services Act 2013 or Islamic Financing 2013; Central/State Government or any other government financing institutions; member's employer; Licensed Cooperative/Cooperative bodies (approved by SKM); Insurance Companies licensed by Bank Negara; or other EPF recognised loan providers.
EPF members who purchase residential house type Small Office Home Office (SOHO) are required to apply manually at the EPF counter.
Additional Requirements For Joint Withdrawal To Assist Spouse
House is fully owned by spouse/jointly owned by member & spouse
Member's spouse is a borrower of the housing loan

What You Should Know
If member has not made a withdrawal to buy or build a house, member may make a withdrawal for any house.
Member is not eligible to apply if:
- The purpose of the loan is to renovate, repair or to carry out additional works to the existing house or for personal purposes
- Loan in the form of overdraft
- Loan taken from an individual
- The purpose of the loan is to reduce/redeem the loan balance of third house
- The purpose of the loan is to reduce/redeem the loan balance of a house bought overseas

--- CHUNK 6 ---
- The purpose of the loan is to reduce/redeem the loan balance of a house bought overseas
The original housing loan balance has been fully settled
If the house is refinanced, the loan balance is calculated based on the current outstanding balance.
Additional Information
Payment Methods
All withdrawal payments will be credited directly into yours or your spouse’s housing loan account when you meet the following criteria.
You/spouse has a housing loan account with a panel bank appointed by the EPF; AND
Your/spouse’s loan with the EPF panel bank is not in the non-performing loan (NPL) status
Your/spouse’s loan account is still active
Your/spouse’s identification number as a borrower matches with the bank’s record
Payment is made in Ringgit Malaysia (RM)

Why is thumbprint authentication necessary?
The purpose of thumbprint authentication is to verify the identity of the applicant for the withdrawal made online.

--- CHUNK 8 ---
What happens if a member does not provide thumbprint verification?
Withdrawal application will be rejected if thumbprint verification (CIJ) is not completed at any EPF offices within fourteen (14) working days.
Does the member need to produce any document for thumbprint verification at the EPF office?
Original identification card (MyKad/MyPR/Police ID/Military ID) for member‘s identity verification.
Supporting documents – Members will be notified via KWSP i-Akaun app or i-Akaun (Member) web portal inbox if there is any additional documents required.
How can members check their application status and payment?
By using
KWSP i-Akaun app (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/mobile/i-Akaun/i-Akaun.html)
or
i-Akaun (Member) web portal (https://iakaun.kwsp.gov.my/portal/member/login)
By visiting any nearby EPF offices
Where can members perform the thumbprint verification and submit the supporting documents?
Members can verify their thumbprint and submit the supporting documents at any EPF office.
