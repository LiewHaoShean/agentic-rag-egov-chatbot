More Than RM1 Million+ Withdrawal
Take Charge Of Your Wealth
Learn How To Apply (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/d/guest/easy-guide_more-than-rm1-million-savings-withdrawal)
The EPF allows members with savings exceeding RM1 million+ to withdraw funds and manage their excess savings on their own.
Requirements
Malaysian & Non-Malaysians
Below 55 years of age
Have savings exceeding RM1 million+ with the EPF
What You Can Withdraw
Members may withdraw any excess amount from their total savings exceeding RM1 million+. Withdrawals will be made in the following sequence:
Akaun Fleksibel
— The amount will first be taken from this account.
Akaun Sejahtera
— If the Akaun Fleksibel balance is insufficient, the withdrawal will be taken from the Akaun Sejahtera.
Akaun Persaraan
— If the amount is still insufficient, the remaining balance will be taken from the Akaun Persaraan.

Payment Methods
Local Payments
a.
Full payment in Ringgit Malaysia (RM) will be credited to your bank account
If you hold an active account with our panel bank and your identification number matches the bank records.
b.
You will be issued a Bankers Cheque
If payment to your account is unsuccessful.
