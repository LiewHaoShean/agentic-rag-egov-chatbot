About Us
The Employees Provident Fund (EPF) is one of the world’s oldest provident funds, established in 1951 to safeguard the retirement future of the Malaysian workforce. Over the decades, we have grown not only in size—managing over 16 million members and total investment assets exceeding RM1 trillion—but also in our purpose to build a better retirement future for Malaysia. We are committed to our mission of helping members achieve a comfortable retirement income.
At the EPF, we believe retirement should be a fulfilling new chapter in life, supported by financial security, good health, and overall well-being. Our mission to help members achieve a comfortable retirement income aligns with our broader goal of ensuring long-term financial stability and a meaningful life for all Malaysians.

--- CHUNK 1 ---
Guided by our values - Progressive, Empathy, Teamwork and Integrity - the EPF remains steadfast in its responsibility to safeguard and grow members’ savings, provide exceptional services, and evolve with the times. Retirement preparation starts today, and together, we aim to create a future where every Malaysian can enjoy well-being during their golden years.
