Pembayaran PCB
Employers
MTD Payment
Submission and Payment of MTD
MTD is a mechanism in which employers deduct employees’ salaries on a monthly basis for the purpose of paying the employees’ income tax.
The amount of PCB can be determined either by using the Computerized Payroll Calculation Method or by referring to the e-PCB Schedule via e-CP39 on the HASiL website.
Submission of the MTD Statement can be made through e-Data PCB / e-PCB / e-CP39, which are provided free of charge on the official HASiL portal at HASiL Official Portal
www.hasil.gov.my (http://www.hasil.gov.my/en/)
