KWSP i-Akaun FAQ (Summary)
What is KWSP i-Akaun?
KWSP i-Akaun is a mobile app by the EPF that offers various user-friendly features to help members manage their EPF accounts more easily and efficiently.
What are the benefits of having the KWSP i-Akaun app?
The benefits of having the KWSP i-Akaun app are as follows:
a. Keep track of your EPF savings and contribution records easily.
b. Boost your savings with Voluntary Contribution.
c. Allow withdrawals for current needs that align with your retirement plan.
d. Protect yourself with affordable insurance/takaful coverage.
e. Update your profile quickly and efficiently.

Can I make a nomination through the KWSP i-Akaun app?
Yes. You can make nomination through the KWSP i-Akaun app; however, you are required to visit the nearest EPF office to perform a thumbprint verification.
Can I submit a withdrawal application via the KWSP i-Akaun app?
Yes, you can submit withdrawal applications through the KWSP i-Akaun app, and this service is currently available for the following types of withdrawals:
a.   Age 50, Age 55, and Age 60;
b.   More Than RM1 Million+;
c.   Health;
d.   Education;
e.   Akaun Fleksibel; and
f.    Purchase of protection products under the Member Protection Plan (MPP) via i-Lindung for yourself, spouse, and children.
We aim to expand this feature to include more withdrawal options in the future to better support your needs.
