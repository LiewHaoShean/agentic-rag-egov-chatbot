Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal
Flexibility For Your Short‑term Financial Needs
Learn How To Apply (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/d/guest/panduan-mudah-pengeluaran-akaun-fleksibel_eng)
The Akaun Fleksibel offers you the convenience to access savings at any time to address emergencies and immediate needs, subject to terms and conditions. It is important to use this facility wisely and responsibly when making withdrawal decisions, to ensure your retirement savings remain adequate. Good financial planning is crucial for long-term financial stability and your overall well-being.

What You Need To Know
Akaun Fleksibel - What you need to know
Supporting Documents
Members do not need to submit any supporting documents. However, an active bank account number need to be submitted to ensure a smooth payment processing.
Thumbprint Verification
Identity verification is generally done automatically through the e-KYC system. However, members may be required to visit an EPF office or use a Self-Service Terminal (SST) for thumbprint verification, depending on the withdrawal amount, as outlined below:
a) RM50.00 to RM3,000.00
- There’s no need to visit an EPF office or SST. Your application will be processed automatically, subject to system verification.
b) RM3,000.01 to RM30,000.00

--- CHUNK 2 ---
b) RM3,000.01 to RM30,000.00
You are required to visit the nearest EPF office or SST if any of the following applies:
- You have no prior withdrawal record; OR
- Your bank account details differ from your previous records; OR
- Your e-KYC verification was unsuccessful; OR
- Non-Malaysian citizen employees are required to be present at an EPF office to submit their withdrawal application.
c) RM30,000.01 and above
- Malaysian citizens and permanent residents are required to visit any EPF office or SST for thumb print verification.
Payment Method
Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal payments will be direct credited into the member's bank account. To ensure ease of receiving Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal payments, please ensure the following conditions are met:
Bank account (savings or current account) status is active; and
Bank account is registered under the member's own name (not a joint/ company account).

When can members apply for Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal?
Members can apply for Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal as soon as Akaun Fleksibel has a minimum balance of RM50 either after the transfer of the initial amount from Akaun Sejahtera has been made or after the crediting of new contributions.
What are the conditions for Akaun Fleksibel withdrawal?

What are the conditions for Akaun Fleksibel withdrawal?
The conditions for Akaun Fleksibel withdrawal are as follows:
a.  Below 55 years old;
b.  Have savings in Akaun Fleksibel; and
c.  Minimum withdrawal limit is RM50.
Can members cancel the application for Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal?
Applications for Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal
cannot be cancelled
once the application has been approved. Members are advised to make an informed decision before applying.

Can non-Malaysian citizen employees make Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal through the KWSP i-Akaun app or i-Akaun (Member) web portal?
Non-Malaysian citizen employees are eligible to apply for Akaun Fleksibel Withdrawal via the KWSP i-Akaun app or i-Akaun (Member) web portal, with a maximum limit of RM3,000 per withdrawal transaction (
subject to terms and conditions
).
For other types of withdrawals, members must apply at any EPF office.
