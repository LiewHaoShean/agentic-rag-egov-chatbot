Message From CEO
Assalamualaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh and Greetings.
Alhamdulillah, all praise to Allah SWT, that the official portal of the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (IRBM) is able to continuously provide service to all taxpayers.
First of all, I would like to welcome all browers to the IRBM Official Portal and heartfelt congratulations to the portal administrators who tireless ensure that this portal is always kept updated.
The role of IRBM as the direct tax administrator of the country is not solely to carry out enforcement activities such as audit or investigation, but IRBM is also responsible to assist the public to gain a good understanding of taxes thus increasing voluntary compliance among them.
Acknowledging this fact, this portal is created with the objective of providing a platform to meet the needs of the public in making reference on tax matters and access the various tax services offered.
