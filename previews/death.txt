Death Withdrawal
Helping Loved Ones In Times Of Need
In the event of your untimely demise, your EPF savings are an assurance that your loved ones are well cared for financially in their time of need, and to aid them with funeral arrangements. We encourage all members to nominate your beneficiaries early on, to enable your next-of-kin to withdraw your EPF savings without complications. The member's dependent will also be considered for the Death Assistance to support them through this difficult time.

Death Assistance

--- CHUNK 4 ---
Death Assistance
The Death Assistance is a gesture of compassion by the EPF to our member’s next-of-kin (under EPF’s discretion). A one-time payment of RM2,500 will be considered and awarded to any of the deceased member’s eligible dependents
(widow/ widower, child, or member's parents, subject to member's marital status).
Note: The EPF reserves the rights to make all final decisions pertaining to the Death Assistance.
A Malaysian citizen or Malaysian permanent resident; or a non-Malaysian citizen who was registered as an EPF member before 1 August 1998
Member’s dependent (widow/ widower/ children/ parents)
Subject to member’s marital status
Application for Death Withdrawal received within six (6) months from the date of member’s death
Member passed away before age 60
