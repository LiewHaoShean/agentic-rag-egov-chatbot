Who Can Contribute
Individuals who are employed, self-employed or business owners can opt to contribute based on their own requirements. In the following pages, we have entailed the details that go with Mandatory and Voluntary contributions. As a member, you are eligible to not only enjoy annual dividends on your retirement savings, but also the many withdrawal options and benefits that come with being an EPF Member.

Persons Liable To Contribute
Any person who is an employee under the EPF Act 1991 is required to contribute.
Among the categories of employees who are generally subject to EPF contributions include:
Permanent, contract, part-time, temporary, and probationary employees.
Any company director who receives wages or remuneration for services rendered to the company.

--- CHUNK 4 ---
Any company director who receives wages or remuneration for services rendered to the company.
Employees who have withdrawn savings under Incapacitation Withdrawal who have recovered and are re-employed in any employment.
Employees who have withdrawn savings under the Age Withdrawal scheme and are still employed before reaching the age of 75.
Employees who have been granted pensionable status but are employed with any employer other than the Federal Government, State Government, or any statutory or local authority.
Employees who have made withdrawals under the Pensionable Employee Withdrawal scheme and are re-employed after retirement under a contract with any employer.
Employees who are not Malaysian citizens who hold a valid immigration pass permitting them to work in Malaysia and are lawfully employed under a contract of service or apprenticeship.
