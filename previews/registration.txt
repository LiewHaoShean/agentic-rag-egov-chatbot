Register As A Member
How To Register
EPF savings offer a structured approach to securing your financial future by providing retirement funds after your working years. Therefore, the EPF Act 1991 mandates employees to register as a member and contribute to their own savings.
Become A Member
There are various ways to register and become an EPF member

KWSP i-Akaun (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/mobile/i-Akaun/i-Akaun.html)
app with just a quick MyKad/MyPR verification and a few simple steps.
Automatic Registration
When the EPF receives your first contribution from your employer, you will be automatically registered as a member.

--- CHUNK 1 ---
The registration will be made based on your name and identification card number as stated on Form KWSP 6 (Form A). The account will be activated once the EPF receives verification from the National Registration Department (NRD).
i-Akaun (Employer)
Your employer can register you as an EPF member through
i-Akaun (Employer) (https://secure.kwsp.gov.my/employer/employer/login)
You will receive a notice with your EPF member number upon successful registration.
Self-Service Terminal (SST)
You can register as a member at any location that has SST by simply verifying your MyKad.
EPF Offices
You can register at any
EPF Offices (https://iakaun.kwsp.gov.my/portal/member/location)
by simply presenting MyKad.
