Cukai Terlebih Bayar
Individual
Tax Refund
The IRBM Client Charter stipulates the period for refunding excess tax as follows:
within 30 working days after e-Filing submission
within 90 working days after manual submission
The procedure however is subject to the information given in the Income Tax Return Form (ITRF) as well as the submission of supporting documents if required for review.
Have not received your Tax Refund?
Taxpayers are advised to contact the HASiL office handling their tax file if they do not receive any feedback regarding the excess tax payment AFTER the period stated above.
