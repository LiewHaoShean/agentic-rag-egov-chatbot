Format Baucar Dividen
Form
Format of Dividend Voucher
Description
Dividend Voucher
(Starting from Year of Assessment 2025)
Notes
Starting from Year of Assessment 2025, a company, upon paying, crediting or distributing a dividend derived from Malaysia, is
REQUIRED
to furnish individual shareholders with a certificate or dividend voucher stating the gross amount of the dividend and the amount of dividend paid or credited.
If dividends are paid in the form of property other than money, the market value of those property at the time of distribution must be stated on the dividend voucher. The dividend voucher may be referred to for income tax reporting purposes.
Notes:
Dividend vouchers in this format must be issued to
all
categories of shareholders including corporate shareholders to ensure consistency in tax reporting.
