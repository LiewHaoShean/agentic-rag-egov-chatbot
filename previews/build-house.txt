Build House Withdrawal
Build Your Dream Home
Building a house is a significant financial commitment. In order to support you in realising your dream home, we provide the flexibility for you to withdraw savings from your Akaun Sejahtera to finance the construction of your new home.
Who Can Apply
With Land Purchase
On Owned Land
Requirements
Malaysians & Non-Malaysians
Below 55 years of age
Minimum balance of RM500 in Akaun Sejahtera
You/ your spouse are purchasing a land and building a house*
Approved loan
from recognised lenders**
OR self-financed
Never made a Housing Withdrawal/ have made a Housing Withdrawal before but have sold/ disposed of the property
Construction Agreement is dated less than three (3) years from the date of withdrawal application
*The cost of the land purchase will be taken into account if the Land Purchase Agreement is within two (2) years of the Construction Agreement

Why is thumbprint authentication necessary?
The purpose of thumbprint authentication is to verify the identity of the applicant for the withdrawal made online.

--- CHUNK 15 ---
What happens if a member does not provide thumbprint verification?
Withdrawal application will be rejected if thumbprint verification (CIJ) is not completed at any EPF offices within fourteen (14) working days.
Does the member need to produce any document for thumbprint verification at the EPF office?
Original identification card (MyKad/MyPR/Police ID/Military ID) for member‘s identity verification.
Supporting documents – Members will be notified via KWSP i-Akaun app or i-Akaun (Member) web portal inbox if there is any additional documents required.
How can members check their application status and payment?
By using
KWSP i-Akaun app (https://www.kwsp.gov.my/mobile/i-Akaun/i-Akaun.html)
or
i-Akaun (Member) web portal (https://iakaun.kwsp.gov.my/portal/member/login)
By visiting any nearby EPF offices
Where can members perform the thumbprint verification and submit the supporting documents?
Members can verify their thumbprint and submit the supporting documents at any EPF office.
