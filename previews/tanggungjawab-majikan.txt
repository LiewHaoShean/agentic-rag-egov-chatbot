--- CHUNK 0 ---
Tanggungjawab Majikan
Employers
Employer's Responsibility
Register an employer’s number.
Make monthly tax deduction (MTD/PCB) from employee’s remuneration based on either the Schedule of MTD or the computerized calculation method and remit the amount of MTD to the IRBM(HASiL) on or before the 15th day of the subsequent month. Employer may only use the e-PCB, e-Data PCB or e-CP39 applications provided through the IRBM’s Official Portal for submission of the statement of MTD or employees’ MTD data.
Submit the employer’s return form (Form E) together with C.P.8D on or before 31 March of the following year
Majikan (syarikat dan syarikat Labuan) adalah wajib mengemukakan Borang E melalui e-Filing (e-E) mulai saraan bagi tahun 2016.
Form E will only be considered complete if C.P.8D is submitted within the specified period. Employers who have submitted information via e-Data Praisi do not need to complete and submit the C.P.8D

--- CHUNK 1 ---
Prepare and submit to the employee a remuneration statement (Form EA/EC) in respect of the employee on or before the last day of February in the following year
Prepare and provide a copy of the statement of cash and non-cash incentive payments to agents, dealers, or distributors (Form CP58) on or before 31 March of the following year.
Maintain and keep records for a period of 7 years and ensure that the records are easily accessible by IRBM if required.
Prepare and submit the following forms (if applicable).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Form CP22 Notification of new employee | Within 30 days after the commencement of employment | Starting from 1 September 2024, the notification of new employee form must be submitted via the e-CP22 application on the MyTax Portal. |

--- CHUNK 3 ---
Withhold any money payable to the employee for 90 days or until a tax clearance letter is received in cases of cessation of employment, cessation due to death, or leaving Malaysia for more than 3 months without the intention to return.
