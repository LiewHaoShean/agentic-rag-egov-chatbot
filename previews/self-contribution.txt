1. What is i-Simpan
i-Simpan (previously known as Self-Contribution) is an option that allows members to make voluntary contributions to your EPF account to boost your retirement savings in a flexible manner.
2. Who is eligible to make i-Simpan?
i-Simpan is only available to Malaysian citizens or Malaysian permanent residents who are registered EPF members and are below the age of 75.
3. If I am a salaried employee, can I make i-Simpan?

3. If I am a salaried employee, can I make i-Simpan?
Yes. Salaried employees can make i-Simpan in addition to the mandatory monthly contributions made by their employer. This contribution is voluntary and can be made based on member’s financial capacity.

9. What does the statement “You have a remaining contribution limit of RMXXXXX for this year” mean?
This statement refers to the remaining Voluntary Contribution limit (i-Simpan, i-Saraan, i-Suri, and Akaun Persaraan Top-Up Savings) that a member may contribute for the year in KWSP i-Akaun app, i-Akaun (Member) web portal and Self-Service Terminal (SST).
The calculation formula is as follows:
(Annual maximum Voluntary Contribution amount) – (Total voluntary contributions from all payment channels that have been credited to member’s EPF account) = Remaining Voluntary Contribution amount for the year
Example:
RM100,000 – RM312 = RM99,688
Important reminder:
Contributions made on the same day through different channels may not yet be reflected in the total contribution amount for the year.

1. What is the Auto Simpan feature under i-Simpan?
Auto Simpan is the enhancement to the existing Voluntary Contribution (VC) facility. It allows members to set up automatic and recurring monthly contributions into their EPF account. Members may determine the contribution amount and the monthly deduction date to help grow their EPF savings consistently.
2. Are there any charges for using Auto Simpan?
No charges are imposed for using the Auto Simpan feature.

Auto Simpan deduction may have failed due to one of the following reasons:
Insufficient funds in member’s bank account;
DuitNow AutoDebit payment authorisation has expired or is invalid;
A technical disruption occurred during the payment transaction;
The payment transaction was blocked or declined by the bank.

VC For Others FAQ
1. What is contribution to other members?
Contribution to other members allows members/ payers to make voluntary contributions to the EPF accounts of their loved ones, such as parents, spouse, or siblings. This feature extends the existing i-Simpan contribution facility, which previously allowed contributions only to members own EPF account.
2. Who is eligible to receive a contribution to other members?
The recipient of contribution to other members must be an EPF member who are Malaysian citizens or permanent residents of Malaysia and below the age of 75.
