# Apa itu MyKad? / What is MyKad (IC)? / 什么是身份证（MyKad）?

## Names for the same document

MyKad is the Malaysian national identity card. Malaysians refer to it by many
names, and all of the following mean the same document:

- **MyKad** — the official name
- **IC** — the most common everyday abbreviation in English and Manglish
- **I/C**, **identity card**, **identification card** — English variants
- **Kad Pengenalan** — the official Malay term
- **KP** — the Malay abbreviation
- **身份证** — the Chinese term
- **அடையாள அட்டை** — the Tamil term

If someone asks "what is my IC number", "lost my IC", "renew IC" or "IC
replacement", they are asking about MyKad.

## What it is

MyKad is a smart identity card issued by the National Registration Department
(Jabatan Pendaftaran Negara, JPN), under the Ministry of Home Affairs
(Kementerian Dalam Negeri). It is the official identity document for Malaysian
citizens aged 12 and above, and carrying it is compulsory.

The card contains an embedded chip that stores the holder's personal details,
which allows the same card to serve several government and private-sector
applications rather than requiring separate cards for each.

MyKad was developed under the Government Multipurpose Card project (Projek Kad
Pintar Pelbagaiguna Kerajaan, KPPK), one of the flagship applications of the
Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC Malaysia) initiative.

## The IC number

Every MyKad carries a 12-digit identity card number, written in the form
YYMMDD-PB-###G. The first six digits are the date of birth, the next two
indicate the place of birth, and the final four are a serial number whose last
digit indicates gender. This number is the primary identifier used across
Malaysian government services, including tax registration with LHDN, EPF
membership with KWSP, and SOCSO contributions with PERKESO.

## Related identity documents

JPN issues several cards in the same family, and they are not interchangeable:

- **MyKad** — Malaysian citizens aged 12 and above
- **MyKid** — Malaysian children below 12
- **MyPR** — permanent residents
- **MyKAS** — temporary residents
- **MyTentera** — armed forces personnel

## Common tasks

Replacing a lost or damaged MyKad, applying for a first MyKad at age 12,
updating an address, and requesting a copy of identity registration details
(JPN.KP12) are all handled by JPN. Fees and procedures for each are published
on the JPN portal.
