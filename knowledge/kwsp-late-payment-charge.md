# KWSP/EPF Late Payment Charge (Caj Lewat Bayar) and Arrears Dividend

This explains *how* KWSP calculates the Late Payment Charge and arrears Dividend
that employers owe when EPF contributions are paid late. The figures below are
the official rules published by KWSP. This tool explains the method only — it
does **not** compute the exact amount. For the binding figure, employers must
refer to the **KWSP1195** notice.

## What is charged

When an employer pays EPF contributions after the due date, KWSP imposes two
separate amounts on the overdue contribution:

1. **Dividend (Dividen)** — arrears dividend on the late contribution.
2. **Late Payment Charge (Caj Lewat Bayar)** — a penalty charge on top of the
   dividend.

Both amounts depend on the contribution's payment due (closing) date and the
date the payment is actually made.

## How the dividend rate is determined

- If the dividend rate for the **current year has not yet been declared**, the
  **previous year's** dividend rate is used.
- The rate used is the **lowest** of the *Simpanan Konvensional* (Conventional)
  dividend rate and the *Simpanan Shariah* (Shariah) dividend rate.

## How the Late Payment Charge rate is determined

- The Late Payment Charge percentage is equal to the **dividend rate plus one
  (1) percent**.
  (BM: "kadar peratusan yang akan dikenakan adalah bersamaan dengan jumlah kadar
  dividen dan tambahan satu (1) peratus.")

## Minimums and rounding

- **Minimum Late Payment Charge: RM10.00.**
- **Minimum Dividend: RM1.00.**
- Any Late Payment Charge or dividend amount with a cents value is **rounded up
  to the next Ringgit**.

## Important notes

- The amount produced by KWSP's online calculator is **not conclusive**.
- For the avoidance of doubt, the **final amount is the amount stated in the
  KWSP1195 notice**.
- Employers can estimate the amount using KWSP's official Late Payment Charge /
  Dividend Calculator (see source URL above).
